Primary Specialty

Fever & Viral Infection Care

वायरल, टाइफाइड, डेंगू एवं पुराना बुखार

Fever is an acute response to viral, bacterial, or parasitic infections. In Aonla and Bareilly district, changing weather brings rapid surges in Dengue, Typhoid (Enteric Fever), Malaria, and seasonal influenza. Dr. Hardeep Singh conducts methodical clinical evaluations and temperature charting rather than immediately dosing strong antibiotics.

High Temperature (>100°F) Severe Chills & Shivering Bodyache & Headache Low Platelet Warning Abdominal Pain in Typhoid Extreme Fatigue

🩺 Diagnostic Approach

Rapid CBC, Platelet monitoring, Typhoid Widal/TyphiDot, Dengue NS1 & IgM/IgG, and Malaria parasite detection.

💉 Clinical Management

Evidence-based antipyretic dosing, oral hydration solutions, day care IV electrolyte support if vomiting/dehydration persists.

Consulting Hours: 9 AM - 2 PM & 5 PM - 8:30 PM

Metabolic Medicine

Diabetes Mellitus & Blood Sugar Management

मधुमेह एवं ब्लड शुगर नियंत्रण परामर्श

Diabetes is a progressive metabolic condition that requires structured medical guidance to keep blood glucose within strict targets and prevent microvascular complications affecting the retina, kidneys (diabetic nephropathy), and nerves (neuropathy).

Frequent Urination (Polyuria) Excessive Thirst & Hunger Unexplained Weight Loss Slow Healing Wounds Tingling in Feet & Hands Chronic Lethargy

🩺 Diagnostic Approach

Instant fingerprick glucometer screening, Fasting & Post-Prandial (PP) blood glucose, HbA1c 3-month average, and Kidney profile.

🥗 Treatment & Diet Plan

Personalized oral hypoglycemic medications, insulin titration if necessary, and customized regional diet guidance for Indian households.

Morning Fasting OPD Preferred

Cardiovascular Care

Blood Pressure (BP) & Hypertension Care

उच्च रक्तचाप (High BP) एवं हृदय स्वास्थ्य

Hypertension affects millions of adults quietly without noticeable symptoms until high readings cause occipital morning headaches, dizziness, palpitations, or cardiac strain. Dr. Hardeep Singh provides consistent BP charting, stage categorization, and modern antihypertensive regimens.

Throbbing Morning Headache Dizziness or Vertigo (चक्कर आना) Chest Heaviness or Fluttering Shortness of Breath on Exertion Nosebleed (Epistaxis)

🩺 BP Evaluation

Multi-positional BP check (sitting and standing), pulse pressure assessment, and cardiac auscultation for murmurs or rhythm irregularities.

🛡️ Organ Protection

Tailored medical therapy combined with low-sodium guidance, weight control advice, and regular cardiovascular follow-ups.

Pediatric Excellence

Paediatrics & Child Health Care

शिशु एवं बाल रोग विशेषज्ञ परामर्श

Children are not merely small adults; their physiology requires gentle examination, precise weight-calculated syrups/drops, and an experienced paediatrician who can accurately decipher subtle signs of illness in non-verbal infants and toddlers.

Pediatric High Fever Chest Wheezing & Asthma Persistent Night Cough Infant Diarrhoea & Vomiting Poor Appetite & Weight Loss Skin Allergies & Rashes

💨 Ultrasonic Nebulizer

Immediate relief for acute bronchial congestion, spasmodic cough, and respiratory wheezing in small children without discomfort.

📈 Growth & Milestones

Regular tracking of child height, weight, head circumference, dietary weaning advice, and immunization consultation.
